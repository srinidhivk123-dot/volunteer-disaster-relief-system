# Volunteer Disaster Relief Coordination System

## 1. Domain
Disaster Management

## 2. Problem Statement
During natural disasters such as floods, earthquakes, cyclones, and landslides, coordinating volunteers and relief activities is difficult. Victims often struggle to request help, volunteers are not assigned efficiently, and administrators lack a centralized system to monitor relief operations. This leads to delays, duplication of work, and inefficient use of available resources.

## 3. Proposed Solution
The Volunteer Disaster Relief Coordination System is a web-based application that connects disaster victims, volunteers, and administrators on a single platform. Victims can submit help requests, volunteers can register and accept assigned tasks, and administrators can manage disaster events, assign volunteers, and monitor the progress of relief operations.

## 4. Objectives
- Provide a centralized disaster relief coordination platform.
- Enable victims to request assistance easily.
- Help administrators assign volunteers efficiently.
- Track the status of relief requests and completed tasks.
- Improve communication during disaster response.

## 5. User Roles
- Administrator
- Volunteer
- Victim

## 6. Expected Outcome
The system will improve disaster response by reducing coordination delays, ensuring better volunteer management, and providing a transparent way to track relief activities.
